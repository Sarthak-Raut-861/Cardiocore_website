## 5. Architectural & Performance Principles

1. **Lightweight Canvas Renderers**: Retain HTML5 `<canvas>` for 250 Hz ECG strips and multi-week analytics charts, avoiding heavy third-party charting libraries.
2. **IntersectionObserver Pause**: Ensure ECG sweep and analytics animations halt when views or modals are closed.
3. **Strict Demonstration Banner**: A permanent top banner across all views:
   > *"⚠️ DEMONSTRATION MODE — Displaying simulated clinician portal for Dr Neha Raghavan. All patient cases, telemetry traces, and consent states are synthetic."*
4. **Zero Credential / EMR Storage**: No clinical notes, interpretations, or patient data persisted in browser `localStorage`.
