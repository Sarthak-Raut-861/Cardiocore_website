## 1. Safety Audit Matrix & Itemized Verification

### Item 1: Demonstration-Mode Banner Persistence

* **Requirement**: The demonstration-mode banner must remain visible across every patient view, during navigation, and after browser refresh.
* **Implementation**:
  - Located in `src/features/patient/components/patient-shell.tsx` (lines 85–92).
  - Mounted at the root level of the layout shell, strictly above the sidebar, topbar, and all dynamic view containers.
  - Text:
    > `⚠️ DEMONSTRATION MODE — Displaying simulated telemetry for Aarav Menon (ID: CC-DEMO-9821). No live bio-sensor or medical backend is connected.`
* **Evidence & Test Results**:
  - **Initial Load (`/patient`)**: Confirmed banner rendered in viewport header.
  - **Cross-View Navigation**: Navigated across `home`, `health`, `ecg`, `live`, `day`, `twin`, `alerts`, `records`, `profile`, `meds`, `appts`, and `messages`; confirmed banner remained permanently visible.
  - **Browser Refresh**: Hard-reloaded `http://localhost:3000/patient`; confirmed banner rendered immediately with zero flicker or displacement.
* **Remaining Limitations**: None. Banner is non-dismissible and structurally locked into the page shell.

### Item 2: Arrhythmia Scenario & Escalation Labeling

* **Requirement**: The arrhythmia scenario and Level 1/2/3 escalation results must be clearly labeled as simulated prototype logic, not clinically validated detection or triage.
* **Implementation**:
  - In `patient-live-view.tsx`, the escalation card triggered by tachycardia simulation now explicitly displays:
    - **Header Badge**: `Level 3 Escalation Criteria Met (Simulated Scenario)`
    - **Subtext Notice**: `Simulated prototype logic only · Not a clinically validated diagnosis or triage dispatch.`
    - **Action Button**: Labeled `Simulate escalation review` instead of a real emergency dispatch trigger.
    - **Interaction Feedback**: Clicking the button displays a warning banner toast:
      > `⚠️ Simulated escalation logged: Prototype demonstration only. Not transmitted to emergency services.`
  - In `patient-alerts-view.tsx`, all alert cards carry the explicit category tags (`Level 3 · Urgent (Sample)`, `Level 2 · Attention`, `Level 1 · Informational`) alongside a non-diagnostic safety note:
    > `Multi-signal safety policy: Alerts require concurrent signal verification, duration persistence, and artifact rejection. Single out-of-range readings do not trigger alerts.`
* **Evidence & Test Results**:
  - Switched scenario to `Arrhythmia episode` in Live Monitor $\rightarrow$ verified pulse parameters escalated (141 bpm, 19 ms HRV, 94% SpO₂) and decision gates flagged `2 of 4 (Criteria Met)`.
  - Confirmed the red escalation card renders with `(Simulated Scenario)` and explicit non-diagnostic subtext.
  - Clicked `Simulate escalation review` $\rightarrow$ verified toast appeared confirming zero transmission to emergency services.
* **Remaining Limitations**: Real physiological multi-signal rule evaluation requires server-side validation against clinical datasets (PhysioNet/MIMIC) before any commercial deployment.

### Item 3: Non-Clinical Telemetry, Beat Counts & Trends

* **Requirement**: ECG recordings, beat counts, signal-quality metrics, historical trends, and patient records cannot be mistaken for real clinical measurements.
* **Implementation**:
  - **ECG Lab (`patient-ecg-view.tsx`)**:
    - Tagged with: `Single-lead ECG at 250 Hz (Simulated) · Strips are for review — never auto-diagnosed.`
    - P-QRS-T sweep canvas is generated by a mathematical synthetic wave model (`getPqrsT()`).
    - Prominent Clinical Notice: `Never change or stop a prescribed medication based on app data. Discuss any change with your prescribing doctor.`
  - **Health & Trends (`patient-health-view.tsx`)**:
    - Labeled: `Every value evaluated against your own 90-day baseline — not a population average.`
    - Variability envelopes rendered with clear visual bands and EWMA smoothing.
  - **Health Records (`patient-records-view.tsx`)**:
    - Demographic records and lipid profiles are explicitly labeled as `Illustrative Patient Profile (Aarav Menon) · Synthetic Demonstration Record`.
    - Disclaimers state: `CardioCore records only clinician-diagnosed conditions. The platform does not diagnose medical conditions independently.`
* **Evidence & Test Results**:
  - Tested 30-second recording timer in ECG lab $\rightarrow$ verified beat count increments synthetically without claiming physiological measurement.
  - Tested range selector (`24H`, `7D`, `30D`, `3M`, `1Y`) $\rightarrow$ verified deterministic synthetic data generation around seed baselines.
* **Remaining Limitations**: When live sensors (CardioCore Band 2) are introduced in later phases, real biometric telemetry must be clearly distinguished from historical simulated baselines.

---

### Item 4: Demonstration State Isolation

* **Requirement**: Scenario changes, medication checklists, consent toggles, and recording history modify only isolated demonstration state.
* **Implementation**:
  - State is strictly encapsulated in React memory (`useState` inside `PatientDashboardProvider` and local component states).
  - No writes to `localStorage`, `sessionStorage`, cookies, or external databases.
  - Re-navigating to `/patient` or reloading the browser resets state to pristine demonstrative defaults.
* **Evidence & Test Results**:
  - Checked off medication doses (e.g. Metformin 500 mg due 19:30) $\rightarrow$ adherence bar updated from 50% to 75% in memory.
  - Toggled consent checkboxes $\rightarrow$ state updated only in local component memory.
  - Inspected `window.localStorage` $\rightarrow$ verified zero keys created for patient records, medications, or telemetry.
* **Remaining Limitations**: None. The frontend state is fully ephemeral and isolated.

---

### Item 5: No False Transmission or Enforcement Claims

* **Requirement**: No interface claims that consent revocation is server-enforced or that alerts have been transmitted to a clinician or emergency service.
* **Implementation**:
  - **Alert Sharing (`patient-alerts-view.tsx`)**:
    - Clicking `Share with doctor` triggers an informational toast:
      > `ℹ️ Simulated sharing: alerts are not transmitted to clinicians without active backend integration.`
  - **Insight Sharing (`patient-home-view.tsx`)**:
    - Clicking `Share with Dr Raghavan` triggers:
      > `ℹ️ Simulated sharing: insights are not transmitted to clinicians without active backend integration.`
  - **ECG Lab Sharing (`patient-ecg-view.tsx`)**:
    - Clicking `Share with clinician` triggers:
      > `ℹ️ Simulated sharing: ECG strips are not transmitted to clinicians without active backend integration.`
  - **Consent Ledger (`patient-profile-view.tsx`)**:
    - Toggling consent checkboxes triggers an informational notice:
      > `ℹ️ Consent revoked/granted (demonstration state). Server enforcement pending FastAPI backend.`
    - Permanent Card Notice:
      > `* Note: Frontend consent toggles are illustrative. True cryptographic access revocation is enforced server-side once the FastAPI backend is operational.`
* **Evidence & Test Results**:
  - Clicked `Share with doctor` on Level 2 alert $\rightarrow$ verified non-transmission toast displayed.
  - Toggled `Share baseline with care team` $\rightarrow$ verified pending backend notice displayed.
* **Remaining Limitations**: Live clinician notification requires the FastAPI WebSocket/push notification infrastructure.

---

### Item 6: No Claim of Authenticated Patient Session

* **Requirement**: Patient routes do not imply an authenticated session or access to real patient records.
* **Implementation**:
  - Route `/patient` is designed as a standalone interactive prototype preview.
  - The login portal (`/login?role=patient`) maintains a clear decoupling: it displays the pending-backend integration alert and provides a direct link:
    > `Explore Reconstructed Patient Dashboard (Demo) →`
  - The patient shell footer and avatar display `← Exit to sign in` or `← Return to public site`.
  - The session state (`AuthContext`) remains unauthenticated (`authenticated: false`, `user: null`).
* **Evidence & Test Results**:
  - Evaluated `window.localStorage.getItem('cc_session')` $\rightarrow$ returned `null`.
  - Confirmed `/patient` can be reviewed without bypassing security guards or fabricating JWT tokens.
* **Remaining Limitations**: In Phase 5+, when real authentication is integrated with FastAPI, access to `/patient` will be gated behind verified session cookies with fallback to public demonstration mode.
