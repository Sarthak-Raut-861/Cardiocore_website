THE IDEA

Any rhythm. Any day.
Anywhere you are.

Most of cardiology happens between appointments — while you are asleep, walking, working, or sitting still and noticing something is off. CardioCore keeps reading through all of it, and only speaks up when your own baseline moves in a way that matters.

  • Learns you first. Ninety days of your own numbers become the reference — not a population average.
  • Refuses to guess. Motion, poor electrode contact and short episodes are excluded rather than interpreted.
  • Says who saw what. Every access, share and export is written into your own ledger.

STORIES OF CARE

What a caught change looks like

Composite examples from the demo record. They are illustrative, not testimonials.

01 / 04

Start with how you're feeling

Aarav, 44, logged light-headedness while sitting and reading. He did not consider it an emergency — and he was right not to.

02 / 04

The gates did their job

Heart rate 138 at rest for nine minutes with variability reduced. Motion check passed, signal quality 0.94, multi-signal agreement 3 of 4 — so the system escalated instead of staying quiet.

03 / 04

The right person, within the hour

The report and the strip reached his cardiologist as a secure link. She replied the same day with a plan and a date — nothing was changed blind.

04 / 04

Continued, not concluded

The episode resolved. His Twin folded it into the baseline, and the watch continues — quietly, until something needs a conversation.

“It didn't tell me I was dying. It told me my resting rate had drifted for two days — and that was the conversation worth having.”
