QUESTIONS

Common questions

Is CardioCore a diagnostic medical device?

No. CardioCore is monitoring support. It surfaces personal physiological patterns and context, but does not diagnose conditions or replace a doctor. ECG strips are for you and your clinician to interpret together.

How does urgent alert escalation work?

CardioCore confirms signal quality, tests persistence at rest, and rules out motion artifacts. If criteria are met, a Level 3 alert provides contributing metrics and lets you call emergency or request doctor review.

Who has access to my health measurements?

Only you by default. Clinicians receive only the specific data layers you explicitly consent to, for the duration of your booked consultation.

Does the device work without internet?

Yes. The wearable buffers raw telemetry locally. Trends and records remain accessible offline and sync seamlessly when reconnected.

THE IDEA

Any rhythm. Any day.
Anywhere you are.

Most of cardiology happens between appointments — while you are asleep, walking, working, or sitting still and noticing something is off. CardioCore keeps reading through all of it, and only speaks up when your own baseline moves in a way that matters.

  • Learns you first. Ninety days of your own numbers become the reference — not a population average.
  • Refuses to guess. Motion, poor electrode contact and short episodes are excluded rather than interpreted.
  • Says who saw what. Every access, share and export is written into your own ledger.
WHY CARDIOCORE

Four benefits

Clinical guardrails
CardioCore is monitoring support. It does not produce diagnoses, and nothing in this portal should be presented to a patient as one.
Never advise a patient to change or stop a prescribed medication based on app data alone. Dose decisions follow your clinical assessment.

Emergency guidance must always route the patient to local emergency services. Automatic urgent booking is a convenience, not a substitute for emergency care.
